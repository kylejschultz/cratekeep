Artwork

Artwork fetching is opt-in. Cratekeep uses the selected MusicBrainz release: exact-release Cover Art Archive front art first, then release-group front art only when exact art is unavailable.

Sidecar and embedding are independent. Existing cover.jpg files and embedded art are preserved unless replacement is enabled. Missing candidate art is a visible no-op and never removes existing art.

Featured artists

FtInTitle moves a trailing featured-artist credit from the track artist into the title before a Library candidate is reviewed.

Use {} or {0} for the guest artist. Parentheses or brackets in this format are preserved. The release albumartist field is always retained. This option controls whether tracks whose artist exactly equals that release artist are left unchanged.
fetchart and ftintitle are explicitly integrated into reviewed Library imports. Other YAML plugins may work in the beets Inbox CLI, but are not guaranteed in Library import.

Beets configuration

Edit advanced options in the generated config.yaml. Library/database paths, core import safety settings, and the fetchart/FtInTitle plugin states are managed by the form and will be normalized when saved.
